FSA

Flexible Savings Account

Room for changing
circumstances.

The proposed FSA is designed around irregular incomes and a flexible approach to saving, rather than a fixed deposit schedule.

Flexibility describes the proposed saving approach. Withdrawal conditions and other terms remain unconfirmed.

RSA

Regular Savings Account

Make space for
a regular habit.

The proposed RSA centres on a regular monthly saving commitment, with intended recognition of consistent participation through Savings Points.

Savings Points rules require approval. Consistency would not guarantee a loan or other financial support.

GSA

Goal-Based Savings Account

Give your saving a purpose.

A proposed account built around a personal target, such as an emergency fund or equipment for work.

The intention is to recognise saving milestones.

Targets, conditions and product rules are not yet approved for publication.

The proposed Savings Points model

Recognising
participation over time.

Savings Points are intended to recognise consistent saving and participation in the cooperative.

They would form part of future eligibility assessment, subject to approved rules and individual assessment.

Points are not a promise of a loan or a published entitlement. No rates, limits or conversion rules are announced.
