Proposed Savings Points

Participation deserves
clear rules.

Savings Points are intended to recognise consistent saving and cooperative participation. If approved, they would form part of future eligibility assessment, subject to approved rules and individual assessment.

Points would not promise a loan or create a published entitlement. No rates, limits or conversion rules are announced.
